# Banoffee Pie Recipe

## Ingredients
- **Base:** 200g digestive biscuits (or graham crackers)
- 100g butter, melted

- **Filling:** 2 medium bananas
- 400g condensed milk (1 can), boiled until caramelized (or use Dulce de Leche)
- 300ml heavy whipping cream
- 2 tablespoons icing sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- **Toppings:**
  - Grated chocolate or cocoa powder

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Base
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
2. Crush the biscuits into fine crumbs and mix with melted butter.
3. Press the mixture into the bottom of a pie dish to form the base.
4. Bake for 10 minutes, then let it cool.

### 2. Make the Caramel Filling
- If using condensed milk, boil the can in water for 2-3 hours, ensuring it's submerged. Let it cool completely before opening.

### 3. Assemble the Pie
1. Spread the caramel over the cooled biscuit base.
2. Slice the bananas and layer them over the caramel.
3. Whip the cream with icing sugar and vanilla until soft peaks form, then spread over the bananas.

### 4. Garnish and Serve
- Top with grated chocolate or cocoa powder. Chill briefly in the fridge before serving.

Enjoy your delicious Banoffee Pie!
